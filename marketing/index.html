COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently asked questions by clinic directors and practice matrons

Clear, direct answers regarding hardware installation, visiting doctor accounting, and statutory compliance.

How does MedSync handle visiting consultant doctors?

Visiting specialists who do not fall under permanent EPF/ETF employment can be designated as "Consultant" with zero EPF deductions and direct session-fee accounting with Inland Revenue Withholding Tax (WHT) calculations.

Can we use our existing Hikvision or ZKTeco devices?

Yes. MedSync connects via standard ISAPI HTTP and TCP/IP protocols supported by Hikvision DS-K1T models and ZKTeco biometric terminals. No proprietary or expensive replacement hardware is required.

What happens when electricity goes out (CEB power cuts)?

Biometric devices have internal lithium battery backups or run on clinic UPS systems. The terminals store up to 100,000 punch records in local flash memory, and automatically push logs to MedSync the moment power and local network return.

Is the EPF Form C3 export accepted by Sri Lankan banks?

Yes. The exported C3 file strictly follows the Central Bank of Sri Lanka electronic return format, ready for direct upload to bank portals including Commercial Bank, Sampath Bank, HNB, and Bank of Ceylon.

How does overtime work on Full Moon Poya days?

Sri Lankan gazetted Full Moon Poya days and Mercantile holidays are pre-configured in the system. The attendance engine automatically tags shift hours on those calendar dates with a 1.5x or 2.0x hourly multiplier.

Can staff dispute attendance records after month finalization?

No. MedSync uses a two-phase workflow: the Draft phase allows reviewing punch records and approving leaves. Once the practice manager clicks "Finalize Month", the records are locked with an immutable audit hash to guarantee compliance.
